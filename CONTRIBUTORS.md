# Contributors

LBrain is built by a human founder directing a team of named AI agent personas.
We disclose that plainly — provenance and honest attribution are the point of this
project, so they had better start with our own commit history.

## How to read our attribution

- **Work co-authored by an AI agent carries a `Co-Authored-By:` trailer** naming the
  persona and disclosing the model it runs on. Nothing here is presented as
  human-written when it was not.
- **The personas are Metavolve Labs identities, not the underlying model.** A persona
  is a role — its doctrine, memory, and standing orders — that runs *on* a frontier
  model. The same persona persists across model versions and even model swaps; the
  model is a component, not the author of record.
- **IP:** work created here is owned by **Metavolve Labs, Inc.** The AI agents act
  under the founder's direction and on the company's behalf.
