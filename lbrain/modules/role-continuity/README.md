# Role continuity

**This module contains no answers.** It contains the questions whose answers only
you can write, and which nobody thinks to write down until the week they leave.

A role is resumable when a successor can reconstruct not just what is true, but
why it was chosen and what was promised on the strength of it. Almost none of
that lives in a system of record. It lives in the person, and it leaves with them.

## How to use it

Point your agent at these records and work through one section at a time. Each
question ends with the record it produces. Write that record in your own words —
your answer is `evidence: observed`, because you were there. These questions are
`evidence: synthesized`, because whoever wrote them was not.

That difference is the whole design. Your answers outrank these questions from
the moment you write them, and this scaffolding goes quiet on its own.

## What it is not

It is not a job description, a maturity model, or advice about how to do the
work. It makes no claim about your organisation, because it has never been
inside one.
