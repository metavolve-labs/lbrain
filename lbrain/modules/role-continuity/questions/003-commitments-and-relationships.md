# What has been promised, and to whom

Commitments are the part of a role that survives the person least well and
embarrasses the organisation most when dropped. Much of what a role owes was
agreed verbally and recorded nowhere.

## Questions

- What have you promised, to whom, and by when — including anything agreed in a
  conversation and never written down?
- What has been promised *to* you, by whom, and is it on track?
- Which relationships are load-bearing, and what does each counterpart actually
  care about? Not their title — what they will push back on.
- Where is there history that a newcomer would step straight into: a prior
  dispute, a favour owed, a subject that is settled and should stay settled?
- Which agreements have a renewal, review or expiry date, and where is that date
  recorded?
- Who would be surprised, and unhappy, to learn about a handover from someone
  other than you?

## The record this produces

One `commitment` record per outstanding obligation in either direction, dated,
naming the counterpart and the date it comes due. Relationship context belongs in
its own record — and note the boundary questions before writing anything about a
named person.
