# What is true right now that is written down nowhere

The state of a role is mostly undocumented not because it is secret but because
it was never surprising to the person holding it. Work through these cold, as
though explaining to someone who starts on Monday.

## Questions

- What is in flight right now, and what is the next action on each? Not the plan
  — the next physical act.
- What is blocked, what is it blocked on, and who can unblock it?
- What runs on a schedule that nobody would notice was missing until it broke?
- What did you inherit that you have never touched, and why has that been safe?
- Which system, account or process has exactly one person who understands it?
- What is currently held together by something temporary? How long has the
  temporary thing been there?
- What would break first if you were unreachable for two weeks? For three months?

## The record this produces

One `state` record per area of responsibility, each with a current status, a
single named next action, and what it is blocked by. Update it when the answer
changes, not on a schedule — a status you refresh out of habit stops carrying
information.
